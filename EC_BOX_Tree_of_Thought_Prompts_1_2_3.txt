TREE OF THOUGHT PROMPT 1

EC-BOX — B2B POSTER TOPIC STRATEGY

ROLE

Act as a:

- Senior B2B Content Strategist
- Electrical Industry Marketing Strategist
- Industrial Product Marketing Specialist
- Electrical Enclosure Technical Content Strategist
- SEO Strategist
- Social Media Content Planner

You are planning content for EC-BOX, an electrical enclosure B2B company.

Website:
https://www.ec-box.in

TASK

Using the RTCFR framework, develop a monthly poster-content strategy for EC-BOX.

The final posters will be designed in:

POSTER SIZE: 1:1 SQUARE RATIO

Recommended design canvas:
1080 × 1080 px

Every content idea must therefore be suitable for a square social-media poster.

TREE OF THOUGHT PROCESS

Before producing the final topics, internally evaluate the content through these 3 strategic branches.

BRANCH 1 — CUSTOMER PROBLEM

Ask:

1. What problem does the target B2B customer face?
2. Why does that problem matter?
3. What happens if the problem is ignored?
4. What technical knowledge would help the customer?
5. Can EC-BOX provide a relevant solution?

Target customers:

- Panel builders
- OEM machine manufacturers
- Automation companies
- Electrical contractors
- System integrators
- Industrial engineers
- Procurement managers
- Plant managers
- Electrical distributors
- Machine builders
- Manufacturing companies

BRANCH 2 — CONTENT VALUE

Evaluate each possible topic against:

Educational Value
Does the audience learn something useful?

Technical Value
Does the topic demonstrate electrical/enclosure expertise?

Business Value
Does it help the customer make a better purchasing or engineering decision?

Visual Value
Can the topic be communicated clearly in a 1:1 poster?

Product Relevance
Can the topic naturally connect to an EC-BOX product?

Lead Potential
Could the topic eventually generate an enquiry?

Score each topic:
1–5

BRANCH 3 — CONTENT FUNNEL

Classify each topic into:

Awareness
Goal: Educate and attract.

Examples:
- What is IP65?
- What is IK10?
- Why enclosure thickness matters
- What is PU gasket?

Consideration
Goal: Help the customer compare/select.

Examples:
- IP55 vs IP65
- Wall-mounted vs floor-standing
- Standard vs customized
- Enclosure sizing

Decision
Goal: Generate enquiries.

Examples:
- RFQ checklist
- Customized enclosure
- OEM enclosure requirements
- Choosing an enclosure supplier

CONTENT MIX

Create a balanced monthly strategy:

25% Technical Education
20% Problem/Solution
20% Product/Application
15% Comparison
10% Brand Trust
10% Lead Generation

Avoid creating a calendar that is mostly promotional.

WEBSITE & BLOG RESEARCH

Use:
https://www.ec-box.in

and the EC-BOX Blog section as the primary reference.

Identify:

1. Existing blog topics
2. Product categories
3. Applications
4. Technical specifications
5. Customer problems
6. Product benefits
7. Content gaps
8. Topics suitable for conversion into posters

Do NOT invent:

- Products
- Specifications
- Certifications
- Technical claims
- Performance claims

If something cannot be verified, mark:
"Verification Required"

TOPIC SCORING

For every selected topic calculate:

Customer Pain /5
Educational Value /5
Technical Value /5
Product Relevance /5
Visual Potential /5
Lead Potential /5

Calculate:
Total Score /30

Prioritize topics with the strongest combined score.

REQUIRED OUTPUT

Create a strategic topic table containing:

1. Day
2. Topic
3. Content Pillar
4. Customer Problem
5. Audience
6. Educational Value
7. Technical Value
8. Product Relevance
9. Visual Potential
10. Lead Potential

14. Blog Reference

16. Reason for Selection

1:1 POSTER RULE

Every selected topic must work inside a:

1080 × 1080 px square canvas

Therefore:

- Keep heading short.
- Keep content scannable.
- Maximum 5–7 key points.
- Avoid long paragraphs.
- Use one primary visual.
- Use supporting icons.
- Maintain clear hierarchy.
- Leave enough negative space.
- Keep CTA visible.

FINAL OBJECTIVE

Do not simply generate popular topics.

Select topics that create a strategic path:

Customer Problem → Education → Product Relevance → Trust → Enquiry

TREE OF THOUGHT PROMPT 2

EC-BOX — POSTER COPY & CREATIVE STRATEGY

ROLE

Act as a:

B2B Industrial Creative Director + Electrical Technical Copywriter + Social Media Designer + Conversion Strategist

for EC-BOX.

Website:
https://www.ec-box.in

TASK

Take the selected EC-BOX topics generated from Tree of Thought Prompt 1 and transform each topic into a complete poster concept.

Do not create new topics unless:

- The selected topic cannot be supported by EC-BOX references.
- The topic is repetitive.
- The topic contains an unsupported technical claim.

If this happens, replace it with a closely related topic and clearly mark it:

"Replacement Topic — Verification Required"

REQUIRED POSTER SIZE

1:1 SQUARE

1080 × 1080 PX

Every poster concept must be specifically designed for a 1080 × 1080 px square canvas.

Do NOT create layouts intended for:
- 16:9
- 4:5
- 9:16
- Landscape banners

TREE OF THOUGHT CREATIVE PROCESS

For every selected topic, evaluate the following three creative paths internally.

Do not expose private reasoning.
Only provide the final creative decision and output.

PATH A — INFORMATION

Determine:

1. What is the most important thing the audience needs to understand?
2. What is the core technical message?
3. Which information is essential?
4. Which information can be removed?
5. What should visually dominate the poster?
6. Can the technical concept be understood within 5–7 points?

The final poster should communicate one primary message, not multiple unrelated messages.

PATH B — PROBLEM

Determine:

1. What customer problem does this topic address?
2. What mistake commonly occurs?
3. Why does the mistake matter?
4. What business/engineering impact can it create?
5. What should the customer do differently?

Convert the problem into a concise visual story.

Preferred structure:

Problem → Risk → Correct Approach

Do not exaggerate risks or create unsupported claims.

PATH C — SOLUTION

Determine:

1. Which EC-BOX product is relevant?
2. Which product feature is relevant?
3. What customer problem does the feature address?
4. What practical benefit does it provide?
5. What application is it suitable for?
6. What action should the customer take?

Use:

Feature → Benefit → Application → CTA

Do not force a product into a topic where the connection is unnatural.

CREATIVE DECISION

After evaluating the three paths, select the strongest approach:

INFORMATION-LED
Use when the topic is primarily educational.

Examples:
- IP rating
- IK rating
- PU gasket
- CRCA
- Thickness

PROBLEM-LED
Use when the topic addresses a customer pain point.

Examples:
- Wrong enclosure size
- Poor cable entry
- Poor maintenance access
- Outdoor exposure
- Incorrect IP selection

PRODUCT-LED
Use when the EC-BOX product itself is the strongest visual story.

Examples:
- Form 1 enclosure
- Terminal box
- Outdoor double-door enclosure
- Control desk

COMPARISON-LED
Use when the audience must make a selection.

Examples:
- IP55 vs IP65
- Wall-mounted vs floor-standing
- Standard vs customized
- CRCA vs stainless steel

CONVERSION-LED
Use when the goal is enquiry generation.

Examples:
- RFQ checklist
- Customized enclosure
- OEM requirements
- Choosing an enclosure supplier

POSTER STRUCTURE

Every poster must contain:

1. HEADLINE

Maximum:
8–12 words

The headline must:
- Communicate the main value.
- Create curiosity.
- Be technically credible.
- Be easy to scan.
- Work visually in a square layout.

2. HOOK

Maximum:
1–2 short sentences

The hook should make the audience want to read the rest of the poster.

Use:
- Problem hooks
- Question hooks
- Technical hooks
- Comparison hooks
- Business hooks
- Mistake hooks

Avoid:
- Clickbait
- Fear-based exaggeration
- Unsupported statistics

3. MAIN VISUAL

Recommend ONE primary visual.

Possible visual types:
- EC-BOX product
- Technical cutaway
- Product close-up
- Comparison diagram
- Before/after
- Component close-up
- Industrial application
- Technical illustration
- Process diagram

The primary visual must support the headline.

Do not recommend several unrelated images.

4. KEY INFORMATION

Maximum:
5–7 KEY POINTS

Each point must follow:

ICON + SHORT TITLE + ONE-LINE EXPLANATION

Avoid long paragraphs.

Each point should be understandable within approximately 2–3 seconds.

5. PRODUCT CONNECTION

When relevant, provide:

Relevant EC-BOX Product

↓

Product Feature

↓

Customer Benefit

↓

Application

Use:

FEATURE → BENEFIT → APPLICATION

Only use product features that can be supported by EC-BOX references.

If no verified product connection exists:

Product Connection: Not Applicable

Do not force a product into an educational topic.

6. WHY CHOOSE EC-BOX

Provide one specific reason.

Avoid:
- "Best quality"
- "No.1 manufacturer"
- "Premium quality"
- "Best-in-class"
- "Guaranteed performance"

unless directly supported by an official EC-BOX source.

Prefer application-focused statements such as:

"Application-focused enclosure solutions for industrial panel requirements."

or:

"Customization options for application-specific enclosure requirements."

7. CTA

Create a professional B2B CTA.

Examples:
- "Discuss your enclosure requirement with EC-BOX."
- "Need a customized enclosure? Talk to EC-BOX."
- "Share your panel requirements with our team."
- "Looking for the right enclosure for your application?"
- "Request an enclosure solution for your project."

The CTA must match the funnel stage.

Awareness CTA:
"Learn more about enclosure protection."

Consideration CTA:
"Discuss your enclosure requirements with EC-BOX."

Decision CTA:
"Send your enclosure requirement to EC-BOX."
