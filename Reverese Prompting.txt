# POSTER HEADING RULES

Create strong professional headings.

The heading must:

- Be short.
- Be clear.
- Communicate value.
- Create curiosity.
- Be suitable for a B2B poster.
- Avoid clickbait.
- Avoid generic marketing language.

BAD:

"Electrical Enclosures"

BETTER:

"Is Your Electrical Panel Properly Protected?"

STRONG:

"One Enclosure. Multiple Protection Challenges."

# POSTER CONTENT RULES

Poster content must be concise enough to fit into a professional social-media poster.

Avoid writing a complete blog inside the poster.

Use:

- Short statements
- Bullet points
- Technical figures
- Icons
- Mini comparisons
- Checklists
- Short explanations

Prefer:

**5–7 key points maximum per poster.**

Each bullet should ideally contain:

**Icon + Point + Short explanation**

Example:

🛡️ IP Protection — Helps protect internal components from dust and water ingress.

🏗️ CRCA Construction — Provides a strong enclosure structure for industrial applications.

🔧 Mounting Plate — Enables organized installation of electrical components.
